# Business Personal Property Tax Recovery

Reduce business-personal-property assessments through asset cleansing, situs, depreciation, exemption, and obsolescence evidence.

**Primary buyer:** Asset-intensive businesses and tax departments. **Evidence:** fixed asset registers, locations, acquisition costs, depreciation tables, exemptions, renditions, assessments, bills, appeals, and refunds.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Asset register ingestion
- Location situs validation
- Ghost asset detection
- Disposed asset removal
- Asset class mapping
- Depreciation schedule application
- Functional obsolescence
- Economic obsolescence
- Exemption qualification
- Rendition preparation
- Assessment reconciliation
- Appeal opportunity scoring
- Evidence packet generation
- Refund tracking
- Jurisdiction asset analytics
